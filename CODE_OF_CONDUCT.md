# Code of Conduct

We are committed to maintaining a respectful, professional, and inclusive community.

## Expected Behavior

- Respect everyone.
- Be constructive.
- Help newcomers.
- Focus on technical discussions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment
- Hate speech
- Personal attacks
- Spam
- Discrimination

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject content that violates these guidelines.
